# Personas

The platform serves several distinct audiences. A capability that satisfies one persona
while frustrating another will not survive contact with real delivery, so each
capability should be traceable to the persona whose problem it solves.

![Personas]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/diagrams/personas.svg)
*Six primary personas served by the enterprise agent platform.*

## Executive sponsor

Accountable for the investment case. Needs a clear line from platform spend to business
outcomes, an honest view of risk, and confidence that the platform will not become a
stranded asset when technology shifts.

*Cares about:* time to value, unit economics, regulatory exposure, reversibility.
*Served by:* AI FinOps (23), Governance and compliance (9), the enablement operating
model (24).

## Platform engineer

Builds and operates the paved road. Needs a capability model with clear boundaries,
automation for everything repeatable, and telemetry that shows how the platform is
actually being used.

*Cares about:* automation, guardrails, operability, blast radius.
*Served by:* Enterprise Platform Foundations (1-6), Lifecycle automation (10),
Observability (22).

## Domain agent builder

Delivers business scenarios. Needs to reach production quickly without becoming an
expert in identity, networking or content safety, and needs the freedom to choose an
appropriate runtime and pattern for the scenario.

*Cares about:* onboarding time, documentation, sensible defaults, freedom within
guardrails.
*Served by:* AI runtimes (11), Workflow orchestration (12), Agent memory (13),
Tool and MCP connectivity (19).

## Data and knowledge owner

Accountable for the content agents ground on. Needs assurance that access controls,
retention and lineage travel with the data into every agent that uses it.

*Cares about:* correctness, permission trimming, lineage, retention.
*Served by:* Semantic foundation (16), Knowledge and data platform (17), Evaluation
engineering (18).

## Security and compliance officer

Approves the platform for production use. Needs preventive controls rather than
detective reports, and evidence generated by the platform rather than assembled by
projects.

*Cares about:* identity chains, prompt and content safety, auditability, evidence.
*Served by:* Identity and trust (7), AI runtime protection (8), Governance and
compliance (9).

## Operations and FinOps owner

Keeps the platform reliable and affordable. Needs consistent telemetry, actionable
alerts, and attribution of consumption down to a scenario and cost centre.

*Cares about:* reliability, capacity, quotas, chargeback accuracy.
*Served by:* Resilience (6), Observability (22), AI FinOps (23).

## Persona expectations by operating model

| Persona | Centralised model | Federated model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive sponsor | Simpler accountability, throughput risk | Faster scaling, needs stronger assurance |
| Platform engineer | Owns build and run end to end | Owns golden paths and guardrails |
| Domain agent builder | Consumes finished agents and services | Builds and operates within guardrails |
| Security officer | Controls enforced in one place | Controls enforced by policy across many places |
| FinOps owner | Central budget, showback | Distributed budgets, chargeback |

{: .note }
> Persona coverage is a useful review question at every phase gate: which persona is
> worse off after this change, and is that trade-off deliberate?
