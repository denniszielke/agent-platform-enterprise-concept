# Centralised operating model

In the centralised model a single platform team owns both the build and the run of the
agent platform. It maximises consistency, control and auditability, and is the fastest
way to reach a compliant first production scenario — at the cost of becoming the
constraint on delivery throughput as demand grows.

![Centralised operating model]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/diagrams/centralized-operating-model.svg)
*One team owns runtimes, gateway, policy, identity, observability and the cost model.*
