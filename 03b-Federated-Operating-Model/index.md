# Federated operating model

In the federated model the central team owns a paved road — standards, shared control
plane, golden paths and guardrails — while domain teams build and operate their own
agents. It scales delivery across many domains, provided guardrails, telemetry and
capability maturity are strong enough to keep autonomy aligned.
